D-5 · How does the seed fit 1 GB of free storage?

This is GAP-2, and it is arithmetic, not preference — the dataset the PRD specifies does not fit the free tier the PRD also mandates.

DEL-4 GAP-2 CUT-3
The collision. DEL-4 asks for ~50 patients × 1–5 visits × up to 2 cine clips × up to 100 frames. That is up to ~50,000 frames. At even 25 KB a frame it is ~1.25 GB — over Supabase's free 1 GB — while CUT-3 forbids paying for more.
