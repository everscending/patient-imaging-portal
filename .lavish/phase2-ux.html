Both palettes come from primary sources, not from screenshots. P1 and P2 are derived from AS Software's own theme stylesheet (#873fe0 violet, #36195a plum, #00c0dd cyan), with the violet desaturated in both — the raw brand violet is a marketing-site colour and reads as a highlighter across a whole application. P3 comes from athenahealth's published brand guide (our brand colors, Nov 2017), which gives exact PMS, CMYK, RGB and HEX values and states which of them pass WCAG AA.

Type: AS Software use niveau-grotesk (Adobe Typekit, licensed), so the build substitutes Figtree — the closest free geometric sans, on Google Fonts. This page is already rendering in it.

Returns 200 even when a dependency is down — the body carries the state, so an uptime check can distinguish "the app is gone" from "the app is up and reporting a degraded dependency" (CQ-3, PF-9).

Annotation. EC-12 in three shapes: storage unreachable degrades to a clear message rather than an unhandled 500; a failed Resend send does not roll back the share link, because the link is the durable thing and the email is a delivery attempt; and the health endpoint reports per-dependency state. Every one of these writes a structured server-side log containing no PHI.
